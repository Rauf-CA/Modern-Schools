    Pricing

    One price per school, in Pakistani rupees. Every school gets its own separate database. Choose the size of your school and how you want to pay.

    • Setup fee, once: PKR 15,000 for Basic and PKR 20,000 for Plus. It covers creating your school's database, your school file, importing students and staff from Excel and a short training session. Plus also covers creating the first parent accounts.
    • Pay yearly: you are billed for 10 months and get 12.
    • Several schools under one owner: 10% off from the second school.
    • Extras: a full training day for PKR 10,000, and data entry or cleanup of old records for PKR 5,000.
    • SMS: your school pays its SMS provider directly for every message.
    • Parent payments: parents pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer and the school confirms. Paying by card inside the app is not included.

    Questions

    Can we use Urdu names?

    Yes. Each student has an English and an Urdu name, and both print on forms, vouchers, ID cards and result cards.

    Does each school keep separate data?

    Yes. Every school has its own database. Staff at one school never see another school.

    Can we bring our existing records?

    Yes. Students, staff, expenses and opening balances import from Excel, and everything exports back to Excel.

    Can parents see their child's fees and results?

    Yes. Each parent gets their own login and sees fees, attendance, results and notices for their own children only. The school publishes updates with one button.

    Can parents pay online?

    Parents pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer and report the transaction ID in the portal. The school checks the money arrived and confirms, and the receipt is recorded in fees and accounts. Paying by card inside the app is not included yet.

    Can the school send SMS?

    Yes, to absent students' parents, fee defaulters, a class or everyone. Messages go through your own SMS provider account, and the provider charges for each message.

    Can we use our own logo and colours?

    Yes. Add your logo and pick a colour theme in Settings. The logo appears in the menu and on printed forms, certificates and result cards.

    What do we need to use it?

    A computer or phone with a web browser and an internet connection.

    What does it cost?

    It depends on the number of schools and students. Send your plan above and we will reply with a quote.
